# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite, confirmed for the clickable mobile-first prototype.

## Users

- **Clients:** people who want meals delivered to places far from the Port of Cotonou, including workplaces. They choose a meal from a vendor's available items, select a small, medium, or large offer, and may add portions.
- **Miam team:** coordinates vendors, menus, order payment verification, preparation, delivery dispatch, and vendor payment.
- **Delivery couriers:** receive assigned deliveries and update their progress.

## Product Purpose

Miam helps customers order meals from vendors at the Port of Cotonou and have them delivered. The initial product includes the customer, Miam operations, and courier workflows. Success includes a clear ordering experience and reliable coordination from payment through delivery.

## Positioning

Customers retain the familiar ability to compose a plate from individual dishes and portions. The offer has three size levels (small, medium, large), with additional portions available to choose. Miam's margin is earned through the fixed offer price.

## Operating Context

Customers order and pay before the order is passed to vendors. The Miam team manually checks MoMo Pay payments. Funds are available immediately according to the user. Delivery charges are paid either with the order or at delivery. Miam then pays vendors and dispatches orders to couriers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The prototype is a mobile-first web app with clickable, simulated workflows and fictitious data; it is not connected to real payments or dispatch.
- Packaging cost should be included in displayed meal prices for a simpler customer experience.
- The three offer prices, included quantities, portion sizes, vendor purchase prices, fees, delivery pricing, cutoff time, coverage zones, refund and substitution rules, and the exact margin model remain to be validated.
- Target volume is at least 50 orders per day; this is an aspiration, not a demonstrated capacity.
- Navette is outside the scope of Miam's initial operations.

## Evidence on Hand

No vendor commitments, verified prices, customer research, delivery performance data, payment integration, or brand assets have been provided yet. Prototype content must be labeled as illustrative where it could be mistaken for confirmed operating facts.

## Product Principles

- Preserve familiar, flexible plate composition.
- Show the full price before the customer confirms.
- Make payment verification and order status legible to customers and operators.
- Keep the first release operable by a small team using manual checks.
- Make vendor, packaging, delivery, and portion assumptions measurable before scaling.
